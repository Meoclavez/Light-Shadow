SETTINGS

Controls

On-screen touch controls Joystick and buttons. Auto shows them on touch screens.
Tilt to move Steer by tilting your phone. The joystick still works and overrides tilt while you touch it.
Tilt sensitivity How far you tilt for full speed.
Calibrate tilt Hold the phone the way you like to play, then tap.
Left-handed layout Joystick on the right, buttons on the left.
Vibration Buzz on swaps, loot, danger and wins (Android).

Playing on a phone

  • Drag anywhere in the joystick area to move — the stick appears under your thumb. A small push walks slowly, a full push runs.
  • SWAP switches souls, ROTATE turns a nearby mirror (it glows when one is in reach), UNDO rewinds a step.
  • ⏸️ pauses, ⛶ goes full screen. Landscape gives the biggest view.
